When a blog post's original hook (a festival, TV episode, movie release, etc.) has passed by the time of a rewrite, don't add a standalone paragraph telling the reader "this event already ended, but..." as a transition. Just drop the stale hook and get to the actual content (health info, how-to, etc.) that the reader is searching for.

**Why:** In the fresh-season blog rewrite of the 대구치맥페스티벌/옥수수 post ([[project-fresh-season-blog]]), added a paragraph explicitly noting the festival (7/1-5) had already ended by the rewrite date (7/10) as an "honest correction" per the site's own TV/화제 콘텐츠 정직 정정 기법 guideline. User pushed back: "이런 쓸데없는 내용은 왜 적는거야?" — nobody searching for 옥수수 효능 info cares that a regional festival ended; it added zero SEO or reader value, just meta-commentary about the article's own premise.

**How to apply:** The "정직 정정" (honest correction) technique is for cases where the mismatch would actively confuse or mislead the reader mid-article (e.g., citing a TV show's ingredient that turns out to be wrong). It is NOT a license to add a throat-clearing paragraph acknowledging that a time-sensitive hook has expired — in that case, just quietly retire the hook and open with content that stands on its own. Before inserting any "to be honest/actually X already happened" transition, ask: does this fact help the reader accomplish what they searched for? If not, cut it.
